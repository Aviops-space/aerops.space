        § Method

        How we approach it.

        01 / Understand

        The operation first

        We spend time with the people who do the work. The spreadsheet they actually use. The email thread that solves the problem. The reason the official system is being bypassed.

        02 / Prototype

        Build the narrow thing

        A small, usable tool that handles one real case. Not a platform. Not a dashboard with ten tabs. Something that fits into a planner's day without asking them to change it.

        03 / Refine

        With feedback, in the loop

        Short cycles. We watch how the prototype gets used, what gets ignored, what gets requested. We cut, rename and rework, then do it again.

        § About

        Early-stage, founder-led, aviation-first.

        01 / Vision

        What we want to see.

        Aviation operations run on a lot of institutional memory and a lot of workarounds. The data exists — it is just scattered across systems and inboxes. The vision is simple: the people who plan, buy, repair and dispatch should be able to see the same picture, at the same time, with the serial number attached.

        02 / Mission

        What we're doing about it.

        Designing and building small, specific tools alongside operators. Honest about what works today, what is in prototype, and what is still a sketch. We'd rather release one well-fitted tool than a long roadmap of promises.

        03 / Focus

        Where our attention is now.

        Narrowbody fleets — A320 family and 737 family. Material planning, stores, rotable tracking and operations-desk tools. We're learning in the open with people who do this work every day.

        04 / Honesty

        What we're not.

        No customers to name. No airline logos to list. No funding rounds, no certifications, no approvals. If someone shows you a page like this with those things on it at this stage, be suspicious. We're early, and we think the work is more interesting that way.

        § Team

        Who's building it.

        A small founding team with backgrounds split across aviation operations and software engineering. We work closely with practitioners in planning, stores and operations desks.
